FAQ

Everything you’ll want to know

What is Zoza?

Zoza is an AI agent that turns human language into on-chain tokens on Robinhood Chain — bridging ideas and the blockchain without technical middlemen.

Do I need to know how to code?

Not at all. You bring the vision; Zoza handles the contract architecture, security, and launch.

Who owns the tokens I create?

You do, entirely. Contracts are deployed directly from your wallet, so ownership and the full supply remain under your control.

What about fees and taxes?

Launching only requires Robinhood Chain gas. Every token carries a fixed 3% tax on buys and sells: 2% flows directly to the token creator’s wallet, and 1% is a protocol fee that sustains Zoza’s infrastructure.

How secure is Zoza?

Zoza is built on proven contract architecture and will never ask for your private keys. As always, do your own research (DYOR) before investing.
